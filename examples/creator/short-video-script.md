# Example Short Video Script

**Topic:** Revenue is not the same as profit  
**Format:** 2D motion graphics  
**Duration:** about 45 seconds  
**Language:** Indonesian

| Time | Voice-over | On-screen text | Visual direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | "Penjualan ramai belum tentu untung besar." | RAMAI ≠ UNTUNG | Sales counter rises, then pauses |
| 3–10s | "Misalnya, toko mencatat penjualan satu juta rupiah hari ini." | Contoh: Rp1.000.000 penjualan | A simple receipt appears; label it as an example |
| 10–21s | "Tapi ada modal barang, kemasan, ongkir, dan biaya operasional." | Modal + biaya | Expense blocks subtract from the sales figure |
| 21–33s | "Sisa setelah biaya yang relevan dihitung adalah gambaran laba, bukan angka penjualan tadi." | Hitung biaya sebelum menyebut laba | A simple calculator animation |
| 33–45s | "Pisahkan omzet, pengeluaran, dan laba. Simpan video ini untuk evaluasi harian." | Omzet ≠ laba. Simpan untuk nanti. | Three labelled columns and a save cue |

## Production notes

- All figures are illustrative, not a real business result.
- Keep subtitles short and synchronized with the narration.
- Use a high-contrast layout and avoid relying on color alone.
- Use original or properly licensed music and graphics.
- Recheck definitions and any tax/accounting claims before publication.
