# Example Creator Brief

> Replace the sample values before using this brief.

- Objective: educate viewers and build trust.
- Audience: Indonesian beginner entrepreneurs aged 20–40.
- Topic: how to separate business revenue and profit.
- Platform: Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
- Language: natural Bahasa Indonesia.
- Tone: clear, warm, practical, not preachy.
- Duration: 45–60 seconds.
- Format: 2D motion graphics with voice-over.
- CTA: save the video and review the daily closing checklist.
- Required evidence: use a clearly identified example and label sample numbers as illustrative.
- Rights: original icons and graphics; properly licensed music or no music.
- Constraints: no claims of guaranteed profit and no real customer data.

## Expected output

1. Three creative concepts.
2. Three opening hooks.
3. Timestamped script.
4. Storyboard with visual directions.
5. Voice-over text and subtitle-only text.
6. Editing brief and cover copy.
7. Fact and rights review checklist.
