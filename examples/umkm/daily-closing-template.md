## Reconciliation notes

- Do not mark a provider payment as settled until it is confirmed in the authoritative merchant/provider record.
- Explain timing differences between payment authorization and settlement.
- Keep sales, settlement, cash movement, and profit as separate measures.
- Redact customer identifiers before sharing this report.
