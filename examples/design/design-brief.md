# Example AI Design Brief

- Asset: Instagram carousel cover and 4 supporting slides.
- Brand: sample independent coffee shop.
- Audience: local customers who are learning about coffee.
- Goal: explain one brewing concept clearly.
- Headline: "Rasio Kopi dan Air: Mulai dari Mana?"
- Visual direction: editorial, modern, warm neutral palette, simple diagram illustrations.
- Layout: one headline, one supporting sentence, one clear illustration per slide.
- Accessibility: readable type, strong contrast, no color-only meaning, concise alt text.
- Outputs: editable layered source plus PNG exports in the platform's current recommended dimensions.
- Rights: use original diagrams and approved brand logo; verify font and photo licenses.
