# Example YouTube Video Brief

- Working title: Omzet, Laba, dan Arus Kas: Apa Bedanya?
- Audience: first-time Indonesian small-business owners.
- Format: 6–8 minute explainer with simple motion graphics.
- Viewer promise: understand three different business numbers and avoid confusing them.
- Structure:
  1. Opening problem and promise.
  2. Define omzet with a clear example.
  3. Explain costs and profit.
  4. Explain cash timing and settlement.
  5. Walk through a clearly labelled sample.
  6. Recap and invite viewers to use a daily closing checklist.
- Thumbnail concept: three separate number cards labelled OMZET, LABA, KAS.
- Title alternatives:
  - Omzet Bukan Laba: Pahami 3 Angka Penting Bisnis
  - Jangan Salah Baca Keuangan Toko: Omzet, Laba, dan Kas
  - Cara Membedakan Omzet, Profit, dan Arus Kas
- Description: explain that the figures are illustrative and that the video is educational.
- Chapters: add timestamps only after the final edit is complete.
- Review: verify accounting terminology, examples, disclosures, subtitle accuracy, and image/music rights.
