## Status vocabulary

- Verified: supported by authoritative evidence in the defined scope.
- Partially verified: some elements supported; qualifiers remain unresolved.
- Disputed: credible sources conflict.
- Unsupported: no sufficient evidence found.
- Outdated: formerly accurate or based on superseded information.

Record event date separately from publication date and research access date.
